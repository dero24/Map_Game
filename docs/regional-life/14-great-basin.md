# Great Basin and Colorado Plateau (Utah, Nevada)

**Covers:** the *Great Basin* (Nevada outside the Mojave: Reno, Carson City, Elko, Ely, Winnemucca;
western Utah's West Desert and the Great Salt Lake; SE Oregon's high desert and NE California's Modoc
Plateau share it), the *Wasatch Front* cities (Salt Lake City, Ogden, Provo) with the Wasatch and
Uinta mountains above them (Rocky Mountain species at elevation, see region 12), the *Snake River
Plain* of southern Idaho (Boise, Twin Falls, Idaho Falls), Wyoming's southwestern basins (Red Desert,
Green River), and the *Colorado Plateau* red-rock country (Moab, Arches, Canyonlands, Capitol Reef,
Bryce, Zion, Escalante, Lake Powell, Grand Junction and Mesa Verde in western Colorado, the Grand
Canyon, Flagstaff, the Navajo Nation and Four Corners, NW New Mexico). Utah's St. George corner is
Mojave (region 13).

**Feel:** silver-gray sagebrush seas between parallel mountain ranges; salt flats and alkali
lakes; red, orange, white and purple sandstone cliffs, arches and canyons with green cottonwood
ribbons; piñon-juniper mesas; Mormon-settled grid towns with Lombardy poplars, irrigated fields and
big shade trees; vast silence and dry cold winters.

**Street and yard trees:** London plane, Norway maple (INV), honeylocust, littleleaf linden,
Siberian elm (INV), box elder, cottonwood, catalpa, black locust, Russian olive (INV), Austrian pine,
Colorado blue spruce, flowering pear (INV), crabapple, purple-leaf plum, weeping willow, ash, sycamore
(Boise), Japanese pagoda tree, and in red-rock towns Fremont cottonwood, juniper and desert willow.
Lawns: irrigated Kentucky bluegrass (Salt Lake, Boise, Reno), being replaced with xeriscape (gravel,
sagebrush, penstemon, yucca) under drought rules.

**Epiphytes, vines and hangers:** few: western virgin's bower clematis, canyon grape, western
poison ivy in canyon bottoms (Zion's Narrows), Virginia creeper in towns; juniper mistletoe (dense
orange-green clumps on junipers); dwarf mistletoe on ponderosa; brilliant rock lichens; wolf and
beard lichens only in the high forests.

**Seasonal looks**

- *Winter:* cold (inversions trap gray haze in Salt Lake and Reno valleys), snow on the Wasatch
  ("the greatest snow"), snow dusting red rock and juniper on the Plateau; silver sage; bare cottonwoods;
  bald eagles at Farmington Bay.
- *Spring (Apr-Jun):* cheatgrass flushes green then purple; wildflowers in May; balsamroot on
  foothills; flowering fruit trees and lilacs in Mormon towns; canyon pools; gnats in Moab (May-June).
- *Summer:* hot and dry in the valleys (35-40 C in Moab, St. George, Boise), cool high plateaus;
  July-August monsoon thunderstorms over the Plateau (flash floods in slot canyons); cheatgrass gold-brown.
- *Fall:* rabbitbrush yellow (Sept), Wasatch bigtooth maples scarlet and aspen gold (late Sept-early
  Oct), Gambel oak orange, cottonwoods gold in red-rock canyons (late Oct-Nov); millions of eared grebes
  on the Great Salt Lake.

### Regional signature

1. Silver-gray big sagebrush to the horizon between basin-and-range mountains.
2. Red-rock canyons with Fremont cottonwood ribbons, desert varnish and the canyon wren's falling song.
3. Piñon-juniper on mesas over cryptobiotic soil crust.
4. Great Salt Lake: California gulls, avocets, stilts, phalaropes, eared grebes and brine flies over white salt.
5. Pronghorn and wild horses on open range; mule deer on Salt Lake's benches.
6. Wasatch bigtooth maples scarlet and aspen gold in the fall.
7. Black-billed magpie on a fence; ravens at every overlook.
8. Collared lizards on slickrock; canyon treefrogs at canyon pools.
9. Ancient bristlecone pines; California condors over the Vermilion Cliffs (rare tier).
10. Cheatgrass (INV) and tamarisk (INV) everywhere they should not be; chukars (INV) calling from rocky slopes.

### Range notes

- Condors: only around the Grand Canyon, Vermilion Cliffs and Zion (and California, region 15).
- Gila monster and desert tortoise only at the Mojave edge (St. George).
- Kaibab squirrel only on the North Rim; Abert's squirrel on the South Rim and Flagstaff.
- Moose only in northern Utah/SE Idaho mountains; elk on high plateaus and Flagstaff.
- No cardinals, blue jays or eastern gray squirrels; fox squirrels (INV) only in Salt Lake and Boise cities.
