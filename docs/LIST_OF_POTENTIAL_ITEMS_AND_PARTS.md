## "Do all parts combo?" — socket graph, not matrix

Every part fits where a *socket* exists and a *trait* makes sense — not freeform. Lantern → pole-top, boat rim, kite tail (all take `light`). Sail → anything that `catches wind`. The honest rule: **no arbitrary bans — physics is the ban.** A sail on a skateboard? Legal socket, rolls wonderfully. A sail on a heavy crate? Attaches, does nothing. Players discover the physics.

## Seats — yes, and it generalizes beautifully

A seat is a socket with trait `holds`. Bench = seat part + legs. Vehicle seat = same socket inside a hull. Your `E`-to-sit becomes universal: sit on *any* painted thing that `holds` — your bench, your boat's bench, your treehouse floor cushion. Resting on your own painted seat could be the tiny loop payoff (view, journal, save point).

## Energy — trait-based, not a grid

Don't build a power sim — make it compositional: parts that `generate` (windmill, water wheel in current, pedal dynamo, crank, solar panel late-tier) and parts that `consume` (lights, motors, fans, pumps). Rule: powered thing needs a `generate` part in the same assembly or adjacent. **Pedal power is the charm pick** — a bicycle stand that runs your lantern string. "Free energy" = you found the right generator card. Keep motors rare so the world doesn't fill with machines.

## Houses/bases — doable, staged

Your pipeline already builds real houses (footprint → walls → roof skeleton → interior by use). A *player-built* house is the same pipeline with the player as architect:

1. **Area** verb lays the footprint on valid ground
2. Walls rise with window/door stamps
3. Roof via your existing skeleton
4. Interior stamps by choice (bed/workshop/studio)

Start with shack/shed/treehouse tier — a full house is the capstone once sockets + physics + interiors are proven on small structures. And it's the natural end-state of the "claim" loop: your painted house on your painted land.

## The honest constraint that makes "anything is possible" true

Primitives closed (foundry can't make ugly), **composition open** (sockets + traits + physics judgment). That single rule gives players infinite invention while giving you a world that never looks broken — and every model you throw at it (Opus, Fable, whatever's next) just adds vocabulary to the same grammar.
